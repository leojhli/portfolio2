A district made together.

The architecture, routes, lighting, Chinese signage, and atmosphere combine original work with these four artists' models. Their textured details help the neighborhood feel lived in.

Asia Building

A Taiwanese apartment and shop block, incorporated into the district's streetscape.

Industrial Air Conditioner

By HusarL · CC BY 4.0

Weathered equipment with animated fans, placed around the district's service spaces.

Old Chinese Lantern

By Scritta · CC BY 4.0

A worn paper lantern brings a warmer, quieter light to the tea room.

Cyberpunk Scooter

By Berk Gedik · CC BY 4.0

A battery-powered courier's ride, parked between deliveries.
