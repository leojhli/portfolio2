Film credits

The cloister

Claustre del Reial Monestir de Santes Creus was drawn by Rubén González Candial and published by Giravolt under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The portfolio keeps the original three-dimensional strokes. For display on the web, the geometry was compressed and a transparent plane and unused shading data were removed. The drawing is shown in pale ink on black. The still image was rendered from the same drawing.

The runner

Assassins-creed-altair-blend by Nexus321 is published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Altaïr and Assassin’s Creed are characters and properties of Ubisoft.

The original model geometry was scaled, fitted to an animation skeleton, and given a pitch-black finish with pale outlines in place of its textures. The opening loop and still were rendered from this adaptation.

Quaternius created the animation skeleton and source movements for Universal Base Characters and the Universal Animation Library, volumes one and two. The free Standard editions are released under CC0.

The captured movement

The climb uses Adobe Mixamo: Sprint To Wall Climb, Braced Hang Hop Up, and Braced Hang To Crouch. These movements were retargeted to the portfolio’s existing skeleton and fitted to the original cloister architecture.

The outline treatment, skin-weight transfer, vault adaptation, Leap of Faith animation, stone obstacles, camera moves and route were created for this portfolio. This edit keeps the original film’s scenery, fast camera cuts and camera-shutter blur, replacing its normal jumping passage with a wall climb before the slow-motion Leap of Faith.
